## Evaluation

Important evaluation measures may include:

- Accuracy
- Sensitivity
- Specificity
- Precision
- Recall
- F1 Score
- AUROC
- AUPRC
- Calibration

## Research Documentation

Where appropriate, each research project should document:

- Research question
- Clinical problem
- Population
- Dataset
- Methods
- Model
- Comparator
- Validation
- Results
- Limitations
- Ethical considerations
- Reproducibility
- References

## Principle

A Medical AI result should be interpreted in the context of its evidence, validation, population, clinical setting, and limitations.
