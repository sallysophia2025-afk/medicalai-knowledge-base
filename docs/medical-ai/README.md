# Medical AI

Medical Artificial Intelligence refers to the application of artificial intelligence methods to medical, healthcare, biomedical, and clinical problems.

## Topics

- Medical Artificial Intelligence
- Clinical AI
- Diagnostic AI
- Predictive AI
- Clinical Decision Support
- Medical Imaging AI
- Clinical NLP
- Medical LLMs
- AI in Healthcare
- AI-Assisted Diagnosis

## Core Questions

Medical AI projects should consider:

1. What medical problem is being addressed?
2. What data are required?
3. What AI method is being used?
4. How is the model evaluated?
5. Has the model been clinically validated?
6. What are its limitations?
7. What ethical and safety issues exist?
8. How should the system be governed?

## MedicalAI Principle

A Medical AI system should be understood not only as a model, but as part of a larger clinical and technological system.
