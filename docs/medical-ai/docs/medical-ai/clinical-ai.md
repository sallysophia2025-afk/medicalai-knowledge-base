# Clinical AI

## Definition

Clinical AI refers to artificial intelligence systems designed to support or perform tasks associated with clinical care.

## Applications

Clinical AI may support:

- Diagnosis
- Risk prediction
- Clinical decision support
- Patient monitoring
- Treatment planning
- Medical documentation
- Clinical workflow
