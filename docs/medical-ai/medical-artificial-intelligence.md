# Medical Artificial Intelligence

## Definition

Medical Artificial Intelligence (Medical AI) refers to the application of artificial intelligence methods to medical, clinical, healthcare, and biomedical problems.

## Scope

Medical AI includes applications involving:

- Diagnosis
- Prediction
- Clinical decision support
- Medical imaging
- Clinical language
- Patient monitoring
- Drug discovery
- Precision medicine
- Biomedical research
- Healthcare operations
