# Artificial Intelligence

## Definition

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computational systems designed to perform tasks that typically require capabilities associated with human intelligence, such as learning, pattern recognition, reasoning, language processing, and decision support.

## Overview

AI includes a broad family of computational methods.

Major areas include:

* Machine Learning
* Deep Learning
* Natural Language Processing
* Computer Vision
* Generative AI
* Large Language Models
* Multimodal AI
* Reinforcement Learning

## Relationship to Medical AI

Medical AI applies AI methods to medical, healthcare, clinical, and biomedical problems.

## Medical Applications

AI can be applied to:

* Medical imaging
* Clinical prediction
* Clinical decision support
* Biomedical research
* Drug discovery
* Clinical language processing
* Patient monitoring
* Healthcare operations

## Data Requirements

AI systems depend on data appropriate to their intended purpose.

Important considerations include:

* Data quality
* Data provenance
* Representativeness
* Privacy
* Bias
* Dataset shift
* Data leakage

## Evaluation

AI systems should be evaluated according to their intended use.

Potential measures include:

* Accuracy
* Sensitivity
* Specificity
* Precision
* Recall
* F1 Score
* AUROC
* AUPRC
* Calibration

## Ethics & Safety

Important considerations include:

* Safety
* Privacy
* Fairness
* Transparency
* Explainability
* Human oversight
* Accountability

## Limitations

AI systems may fail when:

* Training data are not representative
* Deployment environments differ from development environments
* Data quality is poor
* Models are used outside their intended purpose
* Human-AI interaction is poorly designed
