# Medical AI Ethics

Medical AI ethics concerns the responsible development, evaluation, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence in healthcare and biomedical contexts.

## Topics

- Patient Safety
- Privacy
- Confidentiality
- Informed Consent
- Algorithmic Bias
- Fairness
- Health Equity
- Explainability
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Human Oversight
- Automation Bias
- AI Hallucination

## Key Principle

Medical AI should be evaluated not only according to technical performance, but also according to its potential effects on patients, healthcare professionals, and health systems.

## Questions

Before deploying a Medical AI system, consider:

1. Who may be affected?
2. What data are being used?
3. What risks exist?
4. How can errors occur?
5. Who is responsible for decisions?
6. How is human oversight maintained?
7. How is patient privacy protected?
8. How is performance monitored over time?
