# Medical AI Ethics

## Definition

Medical AI ethics concerns the responsible development, evaluation, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence in medical and healthcare contexts.

## Core Principles

### Patient Safety

AI systems should be evaluated for foreseeable risks and potential harms.

### Privacy

Medical data may contain sensitive information and should be handled appropriately.

### Fairness

AI systems should be assessed for potential differences in performance across relevant populations.

### Transparency

Users should understand the role and limitations of AI systems.

### Human Oversight

AI should not automatically replace appropriate human judgment in contexts where human oversight is required.

### Accountability

Responsibilities for AI system development, deployment, monitoring, and use should be clearly defined.

## Ethical Risk Areas

- Algorithmic bias
- Privacy
- Data misuse
- Automation bias
- Overreliance
- Hallucination
- Lack of transparency
- Poor generalization
- Unsafe deployment

## Generative AI

Generative AI introduces additional concerns including:

- Hallucinated information
- Fabricated citations
- Inconsistent outputs
- Privacy risks
- Prompt sensitivity
- Unclear provenance
