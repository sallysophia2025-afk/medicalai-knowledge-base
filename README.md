## 🔬 Research Philosophy

Medical AI should not be evaluated only by whether a model produces a high numerical performance.

A responsible evaluation considers:

```text
Clinical Problem
      ↓
Data
      ↓
Model
      ↓
Validation
      ↓
Performance
      ↓
Clinical Utility
      ↓
Safety
      ↓
Bias & Fairness
      ↓
Generalizability
      ↓
Monitoring
```
